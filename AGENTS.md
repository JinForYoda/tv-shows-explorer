A React app for finding TV shows through the TVmaze API, built on Simple Architecture.

  literally. Don't invent a rule it doesn't have; ask.
- The domain and architecture language is in [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md). Use its terms in code, commits and
  discussions.
- Requirements: [docs/requirements/01-tv-shows-explorer.md](docs/requirements/01-tv-shows-explorer.md).
